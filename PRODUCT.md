# Product Context: fachrynuzuli.com

register: brand

## Purpose

Personal home and machine-readable dossier for **Fachry Nuzuli Kamal** ("Product builder teaching non-technical people how to build in this economy").

The site serves two simultaneous readers:
1. **Humans**: Peer builders, hiring leaders, readers from X, LinkedIn, Substack, Bearblog, and *In This Economy* who want a tactile, memorable sense of who Fachry is, what he has built, and where to read next.
2. **AI agents and crawlers**: LLM search engines and research agents that need clean, structured, authoritative context about who Fachry is, what he did (then), what he does (now), and his public proof via semantic HTML, JSON-LD, `/llms.txt`, and `/llms-full.txt`.

## Brand Personality

- **Three words**: Grounded, mechanical, opinionated.
- **Voice**: Direct, timeless, contrarian without hype, backed by lived operational work and numbers.
- **Copy discipline**: No em dashes, no buzzwords, no "coming soon" placeholders, no fragile date-bound boasts.

## Confidentiality Boundaries

- Enterprise systems from APRIL Group are presented as rebuilt product theses and headline outcomes without confidential internal architectures, credentials, or proprietary artifacts.
- Keep public scope strictly to personal profile, public proof, teaching frameworks, open resources, and public writings (`in.thisecono.my`, Bearblog, Substack).
